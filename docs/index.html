Welcome to Taper

I built this little bad habit tracker to do bad things less.

Pick what you're counting, like cigs, then log each time you do that thing. After a few days of logs, once Taper has a sense of how often you do it, it makes a plan for weaning off.

Your data is stored locally on your phone and goes away when you clear your cookies.

On iPhone, add Taper to your Home Screen before you start: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Safari clears a website's data after 7 days without a visit, and the Home Screen app keeps it.
